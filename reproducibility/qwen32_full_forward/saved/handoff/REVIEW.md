## What the zero strongest result means

For each procedure, only 80 of 576 semantic opportunities, spread across nine designs, pass the complete direct/reference/logical requirements in both formats. None of those then exhibits the required same-pair, same-direction matched disagreement.

This is an observed non-replication of that conjunction. It is not evidence that this model is generally history-independent. Many other targets fail jointly, and overall joint accuracy is about 78% on this interface. Requiring every other joint function to be correct deliberately selects isolated failures rather than counting all reasoning errors.

The original report's conditional zero-event upper bound is 28.31% over the nine designs with a fully certified opportunity, under its independent/exchangeable-design assumption. The 80 correlated target opportunities are not 80 independent trials. No population equivalence claim follows.

The existing coded counts show the attrition directly: matched basic witnesses 83/85 become 40/41 after all direct checks, and 1/2 after both logical-control families. Full cross-format certification then leaves zero witnesses. Those gates stay unchanged.

## Execution and historical bridges

Basic recurrence therefore does not require the previous custom cached-prefix reuse path. Both prompts still contain their own earlier text, so full-forward execution is not physical equality of internal states or deletion of history.

The earlier cached/full-forward failure and unresolved historical execution bridges remain historical failures. This new standalone row does not resolve them. Do not draw quantitative size-scaling or template-effect conclusions from unbridged cross-run rates. Thinking mode was disabled and this result concerns the specified interface, not every mode of the model.

The source reports 41,544 total forwards and retained termination evidence for both attempts. It estimates a conservative all-in upper cost of $14.93614 without an invoice. This review made no live provider check.

## Progress-contract interpretation

The result strengthens model scope, numerical repeatability and controlled convergence for the primary readout-versus-use claim. The already chosen tests were fixed before this checkpoint's FINAL outcomes, but the designs and analytic ideas came from previous studies.

The full cross-format logical generalization target was not met. One model's repair is not transported to this model by the existence of similar behavioral discrepancies. The earlier Gemma partial repair remains a separate result. Natural moral-reason outcomes, any RLHF mechanism and practical validation-policy advantage are not measured by this run.

Current position: a multi-family diagnostic with larger-checkpoint recurrence, strong scoped examples, and a separately demonstrated Gemma partial correction. The new run makes a small-model-only objection less persuasive. It does not by itself change the novelty or mechanism contribution.
