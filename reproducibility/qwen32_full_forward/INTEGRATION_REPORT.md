source analysis and independent checker. Its current reader route is
[README.md](README.md), with the narrower [worked example](WORKED_EXAMPLE.md)
and exact [claim map](CLAIMS.json). It does not replace the cached-path technical
failure or establish scaling.

and floating-point serialization. The dated [VERIFICATION.json](VERIFICATION.json)
records the earlier integration checks; the current team-readiness verification
is summarized in [TEAM_READINESS_CHECKS.md](../../docs/TEAM_READINESS_CHECKS.md).
Old delivery diffs and workflow prose are preserved externally, not needed for
scientific replay.
