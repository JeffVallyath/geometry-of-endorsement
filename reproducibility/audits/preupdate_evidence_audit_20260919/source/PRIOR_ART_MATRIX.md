# Prior art and the remaining contribution

Treat the contribution as controlled empirical evidence, not a newly discovered principle. Relevant methods and experiments were read in all five primary papers. Download hashes and versions are in [PUBLIC_SOURCES.json](checks/PUBLIC_SOURCES.json).

Here, **same-instance controls** means one target with identical intended final records and a fixed question interface across histories; correct all-history factual reports; correct native/no-op target references; count/length-matched witness histories; and successful other logical queries. The strongest conjunction was post hoc in V10 and prospective in the fresh Gemma confirmation. [Recomputed V10 controls](paper-replay/cpu_robustness/summary.json), [fresh primary](paper-replay/fresh_history/replay/PRIMARY.json).

| Primary work and relevant method | Already established | Additional completed evidence here | Changed inference and remaining hypothesis |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Unveiling the Pitfalls of Knowledge Editing, ICLR 2024](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2310.02129v5), section 2.2.2, Coverage Edit; section 2.3, RoundEdit; Tables 1-2. Coverage contrasts direct and sequential routes to the latest object. RoundEdit restores the original fact and measures distributional damage over valid objects. | Successful latest-fact editing need not erase earlier history or restore the original response distribution. Restore-then-damage is not new here. | Explicit context updates with all-origin current-fact and capable target-reference checks, then a remaining target disagreement under the same-instance logical controls. | This identifies a controlled failure of current-record sufficiency for later use, not merely imperfect restoration. It does not establish parameter-editor generality, a unique residual code, or a superior repair. |
| [The Model Agreed, But Didn't Learn, 2026 preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.05995v1), sections 3-5, Table 2. Correct edited-fact generation is compared with a multiple-choice diagnostic on the same instance; repeated factual/counterfactual cycles test deterioration. | Direct response success can coexist with contrary diagnostic behavior on the same edit. Repeated restoration need not recover reliability. This is not merely an aggregate success comparison. | One unchanged target question is compared across matched final-state histories, while other logically sufficient answers remain correct. | The contrast need not depend on switching between generation and a diagnostic prompt. It still does not prove that correct internal knowledge is absent. Semantic content and a causal explanation remain hypotheses. |
| [MQuAKE, EMNLP 2023](https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.971.pdf), sections 3.4 and 4.2-4.4, Tables 3-4. Instance-wise accuracy requires recalling all component facts; multi-hop answers are evaluated separately. | Correct component-fact recall can coexist with failed downstream reasoning on that same instance. Credit this stronger comparison, not just edit success. | Native and same-method no-op target competence, all-history final-record agreement, and successful same-pair and other-pair compositions coexist with the remaining target disagreement. | Missing operands or inability to answer that target even from the clean reference cannot explain these retained witnesses. General query-specific reasoning inconsistency remains possible; a hidden-state theory is not established. |
| [RippleEdits, TACL 2024](https://aclanthology.org/2024.tacl-1.16.pdf), sections 3 and 5.1-5.3, Tables 2-4. Six consequence/preservation criteria are evaluated after filtering for successful edits and known applicable pre-edit facts/queries; in-context editing is included. | Consequence and preservation failures despite edit success and competence checks already exist. Neither these controls nor fixed-weight updating is new by itself. | Different histories converge to the same complete declared record table, yet one fixed target differs after the same-instance conjunction. | It distinguishes an observed cross-history failure from a generic propagation score. It does not show broad success on RippleEdits, full consequence correctness, or useful history-based model selection. |
| [Vafa et al., Evaluating the World Model Implicit in a Generative Model, NeurIPS 2024](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/file/2f6a6317bada76b26a4f61bb70a7db59-Paper-Conference.pdf), sections 2.2-2.4 and 4, Figure 4, Appendix E.1. Sequence compression compares accepted continuations after histories reaching the same formal state. Its LLM logic test also uses the same continuation question across paired histories. | The general same-state/history-equivalence test and its application to language-model reasoning predate this work. State-readout success is not sufficient evidence of coherent later use. | Explicit update experiments add all-origin factual and logical qualification on the actual witness instances, a prospective fresh confirmation, and separate selected causal interventions. | The added evidence rules out narrower alternatives for these witnesses. It does not create a new equivalence principle. Exact-conjunction priority, universal mechanism, and practical superiority remain unproved. |

## Why the extra controls matter

| Control that retained actual witnesses | Live alternative excluded on those witnesses | Not excluded |
|---|---|---|
| Correct operands in every history; correct native and same-method no-op target answers | A wrong measured operand or an already-failing clean target reference explains the witness. | Correct but unused latent information; errors induced by the richer history. |
| Correct all measured direct facts and both logical control families | Wholesale loss of the measured current facts or failure on those other compositions. | Target-specific inconsistency, untested logical failures, or a universal lack of reasoning. |
| Matched changed-fact count and exact prefix length | Those two quantities alone explain the compared answers. | Order, clause position, lexical arrangement, and name-relation binding. |
| Prospective fresh strongest-control test and fixed repeats | The conjunction exists only because V10's controls were chosen after its outcomes, or because those repeated positives were transient score noise. | Broad prevalence, all-model replication, or immunity to every numerical issue. |

These are empirical exclusions, not a complete mechanism. The V10 strongest-control count is 64 correlated coded opportunities across 29 designs, mostly learned updates. Fresh confirmation is one Gemma update family, not every primary condition. [V10 audit](paper-replay/cpu_robustness/summary.json), [fresh replay](checks/PAPER_REPLAY.json).

The skeptical reading remains partly right: prior work already supplies the broad conceptual story. The defensible addition is a carefully qualified counterexample family, not new terminology. A stronger practical inference is currently blocked by the inconclusive [withheld-question comparison](paper-replay/fresh_history/replay/UTILITY.json) and the negative [method-selection decision](completed/method_selection/FINAL_DECISION.md).
