## Verification limits and disposition

The independent review's Linux replay failure is a rendering-byte mismatch. Windows regenerated both SVGs exactly. Converting CRLF to LF on both sides yields exact content equality; changing a plotted number still fails. This audit simulated LF serialization on Windows and inspected the retained Linux traceback. It did not execute a new Linux replay. Frozen files and manifests were not changed. [Evidence](checks/SVG_PORTABILITY.json), [separate minimal fix proposal](PORTABLE_RENDERING_FIX.md).

The three existing paper CPU interfaces passed. The robustness and fresh-history interfaces reconstruct saved-score results; the compact primary interface reconstructs summaries. No replay produces new neural observations. The four other completed deliveries passed closed-inventory hash verification before report inspection. Their reported causal/breadth findings were source-checked, not newly rerun as full tensor or neural experiments. Selected report copies retain links to dependencies in their original ZIPs. [Paper checks](checks/PAPER_REPLAY.json), [completed intake](checks/COMPLETED_INTAKE.json).

The strongest objection is that the broad story combines already-known consequence failures and same-state history sensitivity. That objection is valid. The remaining contribution is the specific empirical conjunction and the alternatives it excludes, detailed in [PRIOR_ART_MATRIX.md](PRIOR_ART_MATRIX.md). It is not priority for the general principle. Lexical arrangement, query-specific inconsistency, and the presence of a correct but unused hidden representation remain possible.

Confidence is high in the reconstructed closed-branch counts and saved-score confirmation. Novelty positioning is a bounded inference from the named primary papers, not an exhaustive priority search. Use the supported claim in [CLAIM_DELTA.md](CLAIM_DELTA.md). No new experiment design or execution is proposed.
