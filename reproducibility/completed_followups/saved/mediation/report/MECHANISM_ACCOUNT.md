# Bounded mechanism account: partial mediation, preserved direct facts

The fixed study completed, but it did **not** recover a complete compact
writer–carrier–reader mechanism. It did establish a useful, bounded chain of
intervention effects on this one selected witness. Every direct factual answer
passed the prespecified preservation rule: 948/948 unique measured direct rows,
including scans and controls, with no invalid scores. These are repeated
measurements on one root pair, not independent samples.

## Where history-dependent information is available

At the post-block-15 boundary, transplanting only the small shared region
426–455 produced donor-directed hard answers in all four direction/format
joint cells and preserved all eight direct checks. Its signed joint-margin
effects were 1.086–1.151 times those of the original cache-carrier transplant.
The full-prefix reference also transferred all four answers; the92 token-different
positions and their union-complement with the small region each transferred0/4.

This shows that an effective donor-dependent state is already available at those
shared positions by this boundary. It does **not** show that the different source
tokens were causally irrelevant earlier: their information could already have
propagated into shared positions before this intervention. It also does not
separate the original learned writer from all preceding backbone computation.

The small-region residual transplant changed the original carrier in the donor
direction (full-carrier projections0.682 and0.668), but these projections are
descriptive alignment, not percentages of a unique mechanism. Recomputing the
prefix can change other later cache entries as well; this intervention alone
does not establish that all its answer effect travels through C.

## Formation of the carrier

Within the fixed30-position region, the full16-component ATTN/MLP donor-output
bank transferred2/4 joint answers. The predeclared selection chose L16_ATTN_ONLY:
it preserved8/8 direct checks, changed the carrier with projections0.100 and0.098,
but transferred0/4 hard joint answers. Its weakest normalized continuous answer
effect was0.0877. No single tested component supplied a complete four-cell effect.
Layer23 ONLY interventions correctly left carrierV16–23 unchanged, even though
later computation/answers could change.

This is localized, partial write-side evidence. The component scan covers the
small30-position region, not every position in the full91-position carrier.
Attention/MLP clamps target the actual residual additions after Gemma's respective
post-layernorms. All ONLY and LEAVE_OUT results, including negative effects, are
retained; the latter are also compared with the full component bank.

## Which components read the transplanted carrier

All128 individual native query heads in layers16–23 were tested. Literal
recipient3<-5 results alone ranked them; the top1/2/4/8 sets and selected set
were frozen before coded/reverse checks. The fallback choice was TOP8:

`(19,15), (18,3), (23,12), (20,11), (21,0), (23,14), (17,0), (21,9)`.

Its training score was0.777757, below the frozen0.8 requirement, so the correct
disposition remains **NO_QUALIFIED_READER_SET**. We did not lower that threshold
or select using confirmation outcomes. Nevertheless, this fixed set's BLOCK
removed71.2–95.0% of the carrier-directed joint margin across the four cells,
returning all hard answers to recipient baselines. INJECT supplied75.2–89.4%
and transferred all four hard answers. Both preserved8/8 direct checks.
These are substantial but incomplete functional reader effects, not a minimum
or uniquely identified semantic circuit.

## Loss and rescue

Restoring recipient carrier values on the selected writer intervention removed
41.7–84.7% of that writer's continuous joint effect. Residual effects remained;
the writer intervention itself was too weak to flip the four hard answers.
Injecting the ORIGINAL donor carrier on the same writer background then produced
4/4 donor hard answers. This exceeds recovery of the small writer effect:
it is not a claim that the writer-produced carrier alone was reconstructed.

Blocking the chosen reader set on the original carrier transplant removed all
four hard transfers. Restoring the same-site carrier-active reader outputs
restored the original carrier scores exactly and recovered4/4 transfers.
This final operation intentionally undoes the block at the same site; it is a
functional restoration control, not an independent proof of natural-path necessity.
Every A–F chain condition preserved8/8 direct checks.

## What is established, and what remains unknown

Confirmed here: a small post15 shared-region state is sufficient for the tested
answer transfer; a tested attention contribution partially forms the carrier;
the original frozen V carrier mediates part of that contribution's effect; and
a fixed eight-head reader set carries much, but not all, of the carrier's
continuous downstream effect. Full loss-and-rescue as a compact, naturally
necessary semantic mechanism was not established. There is no universal SHS,
old-fact-neuron, unique/minimum-circuit, or Ripple/CSP80 generalization claim.
