# Rights and source notices

Original third-party notices are retained with their components. The study
index resolves their included paths. Model and dataset revision identifiers
remain attached to their scientific inputs. Base-model weights and font files
are not distributed.

Synthetic community records describe fictional people. MQuAKE records include
counterfactual stipulations about public entities. They are benchmark inputs,
not factual assertions about those people or organizations.

AI tools assisted this package's software and documentation preparation.
Recorded model calls remain identified as experimental observations.
Operational identifiers and personal paths are excluded from the final public
projection. Anonymization does not guarantee that the research is unrecognizable.
