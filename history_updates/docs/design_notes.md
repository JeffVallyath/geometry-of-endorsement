# Design and interpretation

Matched-history tests diagnose a specific failure under their eligibility checks.
They do not estimate its frequency in ordinary use. Public benchmark facts are
supplied in context, and these experiments do not evaluate weight-editing methods.

The stricter synthetic controls were selected after inspecting the initial
results. The prospective panel applied them to new cases with the controls
fixed. Cache sites were selected on a small number of disagreement cases.
Reserved-case failures and reader-head limits remain in the component results.

These measurements concern the tested cache regions and selected cases. They
do not show that every internal repair fails or that the model found a new
circuit after suppression. Internal measurement is a separate possible research
direction from internal repair.

The external-state repair was developed after the public evaluation cohort had
been inspected. Training uses other benchmark cases with shared relations and
entities. The training-exposure join records overlapping control questions.
Equal dictionaries give equal reader inputs by construction; correctness is
measured separately.

The common-state reader comparison matches source cases, example order and
optimizer budget. The supervision targets differ between ranking, answer
generation and operation generation. Their near-tie is retained without an
equivalence claim or a claim that scoring is uniquely superior.

The corrected GMeLLo comparison uses few-shot Qwen, while the repair scorers
are trained. It compares whole systems with matched available facts. It is a
documented local-graph reimplementation, not a reproduction of the authors'
full-Wikidata system. GMeLLo is itself an external-state method.

Revision 0 was selected after all three development settings had been inspected.
It had the highest recorded primary graph coverage. The original development
gate remained unqualified. A documented amendment removed its numerical
coverage veto before main inference, without another development sweep or
retuning. All three development outcomes remain available. The evaluation
cohort was already exposed; it is not a new untouched confirmation sample.

The earlier GMeLLo implementation's 35/128 result remains a separately labeled,
superseded observation. The corrected CORE, FULL and secondary TEXT results
are the GMeLLo rows in the final paper's Table 13. No earlier row is relabeled
with a new result.
